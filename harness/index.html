One Does Not Simply Walk Into Mordor

Boromir of Gondor addressed the Council of Elrond with a warning. "Its black gates are guarded by more than just orcs," he said. There is evil there that does not sleep, and the Great Eye is ever watchful.

It is a barren wasteland, riddled with fire and ash and dust. The very air you breathe is a poisonous fume. Not with ten thousand men could you do this. It is folly!

Mr. Frodo Baggins volunteered anyway, at 3:30 p.m. on 25 October 3018, and the Fellowship of nine set out from Rivendell soon after.

The journey covered roughly 1,350 miles, cost the company dearly, and took about six months. Dr. Gandalf's estimate was $3.50 in pipe-weed.

In the end, Mount Doom claimed the Ring, and the Shadow passed from the land.
